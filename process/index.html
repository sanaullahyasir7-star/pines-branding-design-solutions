Process · Project stages

A considered path from idea to production.

A project can move from design and mockups through sampling to production. The scope, approvals and timing are discussed for each brief.

01 / ALIGN

Design

Set a clear direction for the project. Scope and revisions are agreed before work begins.

You provide

Project goals, references and known requirements.

You receive

A design direction to review together.

02 / VISUALIZE

Mockups

Review how the design may appear in context.

You provide

Feedback and decisions on the design direction.

You receive

A mockup to review in context.

03 / REVIEW

Sampling

Use a sample stage to review agreed details before production.

You provide

Approval notes and any changes.

You receive

A sample to review against the agreed brief.

04 / MAKE

Production

Move into production after scope, specifications and approvals are agreed.

You provide

Final approved artwork and written production approval.

You receive

Production against the approved specifications and agreed plan.

Before starting

Bring what you know.

Even an early idea is enough to start a conversation. Share your intended product, approximate quantity, deadline and any artwork or reference files available.
