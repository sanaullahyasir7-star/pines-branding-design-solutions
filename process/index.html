Process · Proposed stages

A considered path from idea to production.

This sequence reflects the four services Pines has shared. The exact timelines, approvals, deliverables and handoffs still need confirmation.

01 / ALIGN

Design

Set a clear direction for the project. Scope and revisions are agreed before work begins.

You provide

Project goals, references and known requirements.

You receive

A proposed visual direction. Deliverable to confirm.

02 / VISUALIZE

Mockups

Review how the proposed design may appear in context.

You provide

Feedback and decisions on the proposed direction.

You receive

A mockup format to review. Confirm fidelity and revisions.

03 / REVIEW

Sampling

Use a sample stage to review agreed details before production.

You provide

Approval notes and any changes.

You receive

Sample details and timing to confirm.

04 / MAKE

Production

Move into production after scope, specifications and approvals are agreed.

You provide

Final approved artwork and written production approval.

You receive

Production outcome and delivery plan. Confirm capabilities and lead times.

All timings, revision limits, sample fees, approval responsibilities and delivery arrangements are [CONFIRM WITH CLIENT].

Bring what you know.
