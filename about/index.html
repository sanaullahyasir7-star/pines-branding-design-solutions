About · Pines

Design thinking, with making in mind.

Pines Branding & Designs Solution is based in Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. It brings design, mockups, sampling and production together for packaging and print projects, serving businesses that need custom physical brand assets.

One connected solution

From visual direction to a physical piece.

Packaging and print bring a brand into the physical world. Pines connects the early design work with mockups, sampling and production in one project conversation.

Product scope, available formats, materials and production details are discussed for each brief.
