PINES · PRIVACY NOTICE

Your privacy, clearly explained.

This notice explains what happens to information when you use the Pines website, project planner and WhatsApp enquiry links.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Information you enter

The project planner and enquiry form run in your browser. This website does not send form entries to a Pines server or database. You can prepare a brief, copy it or download it on your device. The website does not upload artwork or other files.

When you open WhatsApp

If you choose “Open WhatsApp,” the prepared brief and the details you entered are included in the WhatsApp link. WhatsApp receives that link so it can open a chat with the representative you selected. Pines receives your enquiry only after you press Send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp handles information under its own Privacy Policy.

If you want to share artwork, attach it in WhatsApp after the chat opens. Do not include information in a brief unless you are comfortable including it in that link.

Hosting and external services

This website is currently hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests font files from Google, which may process technical request information. See Google’s Privacy Policy. No analytics or advertising tools are configured on this website.

Questions about an enquiry

After you send a WhatsApp enquiry, Pines handles it through its business messaging process. Contact Pines to ask how your enquiry is used or retained. The website does not specify a retention period for messages sent to Pines.
