# Pines site design notes

- Preserve the supplied Pines logo and green/cream identity; keep the existing editorial serif headline treatment and restrained italic accent.
- Use a calm studio voice, square-cornered layouts, clear dividers and spacious editorial grids. Keep pine imagery limited to the existing logo/brand cues.
- Keep the four offered stages (design, mockups, sampling, production) as the known service summary; treat product examples, capabilities and workflow details as client-confirmation items.
- Separate service categories (what a client may ask Pines to make) from process stages (what happens during a project).
- Use labeled illustrative assets and abstract placeholders until Pines supplies approved portfolio and production photography.
- Keep testimonials, business details and sustainability statements empty or clearly marked until supported by client-approved facts.
- Use restrained reveals, a slow marquee, underline hover states, expandable FAQs and a mobile menu; all motion respects reduced-motion settings.
- Make the quote form ready for connection but do not imply a message was sent while no verified provider endpoint is configured.
